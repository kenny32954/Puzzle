ORAL HISTORY TRANSCRIPT
BELLWEATHER PUBLIC LIBRARY

Interviewee: June Keller
Interviewer: Samuel Reed

KELLER: We moved to North Street when I was eight. The bakery was already there then.

REED: Do you remember the old records building?

KELLER: Mostly the steps. They were very tall when I was little. My mother hated carrying boxes up them.

REED: Did you ever go inside?

KELLER: Once for a school project. I remember the smell of dust and floor polish more than anything else.

End excerpt.
