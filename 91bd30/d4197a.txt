Dear Orla,

Thank you for the flowers and for pretending the retirement luncheon was a surprise.

I found the old photograph of the three of us outside the annex. You were right about the raincoat. I was wrong about the year.

Ruth says the garden club still wants the folding screens we stored in the back room. If you have the patience to sort them, they are theirs.

I am keeping the little brass desk bell. After thirty-one years I think I have earned it.

Elaine
