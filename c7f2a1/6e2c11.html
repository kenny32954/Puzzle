THE HARROW COUNTY RECORD

THURSDAY, JUNE 18, 1987 · 35¢

North Annex Reopens After Eleven-Year Closure

The North Annex of the Bellweather Civic Repository reopened Tuesday following extensive repairs to its roof, east stairwell, and climate-control system. The building has remained closed to the public since 1976.

Repository director Elaine Mercer said the renovated annex will hold regional maps, school registers, municipal photographs, radio transcripts, and private collections donated by area families.

The opening ceremony began at 10:45 a.m. and included remarks from Mercer, Councilman Theodore Venn, architect Orla Morrow, and retired custodian Owen Bell.

Visitors reported long lines through the west entrance during the afternoon. The reading room reached capacity shortly after 2 p.m.

New exhibition cases were installed in the central gallery along with updated lighting and seating.
